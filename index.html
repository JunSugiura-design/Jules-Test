Our Philosophy

At Lumière, we believe that bread is the soul of a meal. Using only the finest organic flour, natural leaven, and time-honored techniques, we bake fresh every morning to bring you the warmth of tradition.

Founded in 1985, our bakery has been a gathering place for community and connoisseurs alike. We strive to balance modern innovation with the classic art of boulangerie.
